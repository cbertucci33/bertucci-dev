## About

Carmen Bertucci — AI builder and educator based in Chicago.

I run and tune local language models on hardware I own, build tooling and
workflows around them, and teach people how to get real value out of AI
without renting a cloud cluster.

This site is where the work lives. I write about what I'm learning,
document the projects I'm building, and share the honest parts of running
AI locally — including the parts that don't work.

### What I care about

- **Local, self-hosted AI** — models you actually control
- **Honest benchmarks** — reading past the score
- **Teaching** — webinars, writing, and content that demystifies the stack
- **Building in public** — the real work, not the highlight reel
