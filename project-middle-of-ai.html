The Middle of AI

A series of webinars and articles trying to demystify modern AI models for regular people, so they can understand how they work and run them on their own hardware.

What it is

There's a huge gap in how AI is talked about. On one side, the hype tells you models are magic. On the other, the technical material assumes you already know what a transformer is and how quantization works. Most people live in the middle, and that's exactly where the confusion lives.

The Middle of AI is a series of webinars and articles that sits in that gap. It's for people who want to actually understand modern models well enough to run them on their own hardware, without the hype and without the jargon for its own sake.

What we cover

This is draft territory right now, but the shape is clear: what these models actually are under the hood, how they're trained and served, how to choose and run one locally, and how to read a benchmark without fooling yourself.

Webinars and written pieces will build on each other, each one taking a real concept and walking through it honestly. No magic, no black box, no hand-waving. Just the ground between "AI is magic" and "AI is a black box" - and how to actually use it.
