## Hello, and this is the work

This site is where I keep the work — running local AI, building tooling
around models, and teaching people how to get real value out of it.

I'll write about what I'm learning, document what I'm building, and share
the honest parts — including the parts that don't work. Most of the AI
content out there either hand-waves the hard parts or drowns in jargon.
I want to sit in the middle: technically real, but readable.

More soon.
