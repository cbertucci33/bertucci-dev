## ClearPing

Self-hosted uptime monitoring done properly — no cloud dependency, full control
over your checks and alerting.

**Status:** Active

### What it is

ClearPing is my take on uptime monitoring that you actually own. It's about
knowing when something's down on your own terms, with your own infra,
without renting someone else's dashboard.

### Why

Most monitoring tools are hosted and opaque. I wanted something where the
data stays mine, the alerts go where I want them, and nothing phones home.
