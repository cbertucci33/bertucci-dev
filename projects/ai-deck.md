## AI Educational Deck Series

A multi-part educational deck teaching local AI from the ground up — how models
are trained and served, what quants actually do, and how to read benchmarks
without fooling yourself.

**Status:** Active — Part 1 (Models) complete

### What it covers

- **Transformers & vLLM** — training and serving the modern way
- **Quantization** — fewer bits, fuzzier math, and what you actually lose
- **Tokens per second** — real throughput numbers, not marketing
- **Benchmarks** — reading past the score
- **Safetensors formats** — the standard for Hugging Face weights

### Why

Most content on running local models either hand-waves the hard parts or
drowns you in jargon. This is the middle path: technically real, but
explained so a normal person can follow it.
